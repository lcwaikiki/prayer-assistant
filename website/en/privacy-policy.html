Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

About this policy

Our apps do not collect, store or share your personal data: everything you enter stays on your device.

pirci company makes lifestyle mobile apps for Android. This policy applies to every app pirci company publishes on Google Play ("our apps") and explains how we handle your information. By using our apps, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through our apps. They have no user accounts, no servers that store your data, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

  • Data you create in an app, such as entries, settings and preferences, is saved only on your device. We cannot see or access it.
  • Device permissions. Some features may ask for access to parts of your device, such as notifications or photos. These are used only on your device for the feature you are using, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn permissions off at any time in your device settings.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you.

How we use information

Because our apps do not collect personal data, we do not use it for anything. The only information we handle is what you send us by email, and we use it only to answer your question or support request. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing and third-party services

We do not share, sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Our apps are distributed through Google Play. When you download or update an app, Google may collect information under its own Privacy Policy. Google may also give us anonymous, aggregated statistics, such as install counts and crash reports from users who chose to share diagnostics with Google. These statistics do not identify you.

We would disclose the emails you send us only if required by law, such as by a valid court order.

Data retention and deletion

Data in our apps stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive this data, we cannot recover it for you.

We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to help you, and we delete them sooner if you ask. Our apps have no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

Security

Because your app data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock and Android's built-in encryption. Our apps never send that data over the internet, so it cannot be intercepted on its way to us. We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

Children's privacy

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You control the data in our apps: you can view, change or delete it on your device at any time. For any information you have sent us by email, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it, and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) or US state privacy laws may give you further rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps change, for example if an app adds a feature that sends data off your device. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also make it clear in the app or its Google Play listing.

Contact us

For questions about this policy or your privacy, email pirci company at pirci.company@gmail.com.
